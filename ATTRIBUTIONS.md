# Data and Service Attributions

- OpenGolfAPI course imports: data includes © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under ODbL 1.0. Imported records retain provider, license, attribution, source URL, and retrieval time.
- Built-in course snapshot: the exact distributable database is `data/coursecompass-open-courses.json`, offered under ODbL 1.0 with record-level source identifiers. Complete scorecards are displayed only where an open source supplies all 18 holes; incomplete sources remain catalog-only.
- Multi-tee scorecards: 166 complete tee sets across 27 built-in courses are retained from OpenGolfAPI under the same ODbL attribution. Partial tee sets are not published.
- OpenStreetMap/Overpass course discovery and geometry: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.
- Open-Meteo weather: weather data supplied by Open-Meteo; model/source details vary by location.
- U.S. Geological Survey: elevation and USGS imagery services are used where available. Availability and coverage vary.
- Firebase: optional authentication and synchronized storage are provided by Google Firebase.
- Inter, Playfair Display, and JetBrains Mono fonts are self-hosted with the application; retain their upstream license files in a distribution audit.
- QR generation uses the locally bundled `qrcode-generator` implementation and is performed on device.
