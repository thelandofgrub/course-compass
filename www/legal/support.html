Support

For help, open a CourseCompass support request on GitHub. Include the app version, device/browser, what you expected, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Do not include passwords, account identifiers, private group codes, or precise locations.

Quick checks

  • Weather or maps: confirm network and location permission, then refresh the On Course panel.
  • Group sync: confirm both players use the same group code and show a connected status.
  • Voice Caddie: enable it in Settings and allow microphone/speech permissions when requested.
  • Offline use: open the app once online so the app shell can be cached.

Diagnostics

Settings → Privacy & Location → Export diagnostics creates a local troubleshooting file. Review and remove personal information before sharing it.

Data requests

Use the in-app export and deletion controls. See the account deletion instructions if you cannot access the app.
