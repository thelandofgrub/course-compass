## Full description

CourseCompass brings practical golf planning, live play, learning, and performance review into one focused experience.

Use the virtual caddie to compare clubs and calculate playing distance from wind, elevation, temperature, altitude, and lie. On Course combines hole strategy, live GPS yardages, weather, green information, scoring, and locally generated group invitations. Track rounds and shot patterns, review handicap estimates and trends, build a personal club-distance chart, study lessons and glossary terms, and use Voice Caddie for hands-free guidance where the device supports speech.

Optional Firebase synchronization links players for live group scoring and carries selected golf data across devices. The core app remains useful offline after its first load. CourseCompass uses free-tier-conscious product controls: no ads, no in-app purchases, and no billable generative-AI calls.

Distances, maps, weather, handicap calculations, and strategy are estimates. Always follow course markings, local rules, weather alerts, and safe judgment.
