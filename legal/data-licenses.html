Download the database

Download the exact attributed JSON snapshot. Records indicate whether they contain a licensed scorecard, mapped/calculated hole data, catalog identity only, or an official identity reference.

The snapshot includes 166 complete 18-hole tee sets across 27 courses. Incomplete tee sets are excluded. Calculated OSM hole lengths are not official tee measurements. CourseCompass strategy sentences are generated from sourced par and distance fields and are not copied course descriptions.
