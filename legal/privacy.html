CourseCompass

Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026

This policy explains how the CourseCompass project, maintained by thelandofgrub (“CourseCompass,” “we,” or “us”), handles information when you use CourseCompass.

Information you provide

  • Player name, profile, club setup, preferences, scores, rounds, shots, custom courses, and group-round entries.
  • Email address if you upgrade an anonymous Firebase account to a permanent account.
  • Support messages you choose to send.

Location

With permission, precise location is used while the app is open to find nearby courses, show live yardages, select targets, enrich course maps, retrieve local weather, and measure shots. CourseCompass does not place location history in group-score records. You can deny or revoke location permission in device settings.

Voice

Voice Caddie uses speech recognition and text-to-speech supplied by your browser or operating system. Depending on the device provider, spoken audio may be processed by that provider. CourseCompass does not store a voice recording in its Firebase database.

Local and cloud storage

Core golf data is stored locally on your device. Cloud sync is optional and uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to synchronize selected profile, bag, round, score, and group data. Anonymous sign-in still creates a unique cloud account identifier.

Service providers and public APIs

CourseCompass uses Google Firebase for optional authentication and synchronization. Weather, map, elevation, and open course requests may be sent to Open-Meteo, OpenGolfAPI, OpenStreetMap/Overpass providers, and the U.S. Geological Survey. These services receive the network information needed to answer a request, which can include IP address and course or device coordinates.

Advertising, tracking, and sales

CourseCompass does not include advertising, does not sell personal information, and does not use data for cross-app tracking. Firebase Analytics is not initialized by the application.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it, clear browser/app storage, or uninstall the app. Cloud data remains until deleted through Privacy & Location → Delete cloud account, subject to operational backup retention by the service provider. Export first if you wish to retain a copy.

Security and children

Firestore access rules separate user records and restrict group updates. No system is perfectly secure. CourseCompass is a general-audience golf utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You may export local data, delete local data, delete a cloud account, disable Voice Caddie, avoid cloud sync, and change location permissions at any time.
