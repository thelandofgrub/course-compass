# CourseCompass Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026. This operational policy is not legal advice.

The public HTML version is `legal/privacy.html`.

## Owner and contact

CourseCompass project, maintained by `thelandofgrub`.

- Website: <https://thelandofgrub.github.io/course-compass/>
- Support: <https://github.com/thelandofgrub/course-compass/issues/new>
- Repository: <https://github.com/thelandofgrub/course-compass>

Do not post passwords, account identifiers, private group codes, precise locations, or other sensitive personal data in a public GitHub issue.

## Data handled

- Player name, profile, clubs, preferences, scores, rounds, shots, custom courses, and group-round entries supplied by the player.
- Email address only when a player converts an anonymous Firebase identity to a permanent email/password account.
- Precise location, with permission, while the app is open for nearby-course discovery, live yardage, target selection, map enrichment, local weather, and shot measurement. Location history is not written to live-group records.
- Voice Caddie uses speech services supplied by the browser or operating system. CourseCompass does not store voice recordings in Firebase, though the platform speech provider may process audio under its own terms.

Core data is stored locally. Optional cloud sync uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for selected profile, bag, round, score, and group data. Anonymous sign-in creates a unique cloud identifier.

CourseCompass also requests data from Open-Meteo, OpenGolfAPI, OpenStreetMap/Overpass providers, and the U.S. Geological Survey. Those services can receive IP address and the coordinates needed to answer the request.

CourseCompass has no advertising, sale of personal information, cross-app tracking, or initialized Firebase Analytics. Billable generative-AI calls are disabled by product policy.

Local data remains until deleted, app/browser storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Cloud data remains until the in-app Delete cloud account control is used, subject to provider backup retention. Players can export, delete local data, delete the connected cloud account, disable voice, avoid sync, and revoke location permission.

CourseCompass is a general-audience golf utility and is not directed to children under 13.
